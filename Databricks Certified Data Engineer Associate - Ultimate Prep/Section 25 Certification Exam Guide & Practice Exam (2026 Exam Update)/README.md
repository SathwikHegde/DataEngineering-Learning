## Top Strategic Tips for Exam Day

1. **Flag and Move On**: Time management is key. Spending more than two minutes on a tricky, edge-case query parsing question can derail your rhythm. Flag the question and return to it once you have secured the easier points.
2. **Eliminate Legacy Distractors**: Databricks certification options often try to trick you with older solutions. If a question asks about high-concurrency 2026 governance or physical organization, options recommending *Hive Metastore* or manual *Z-Ordering/Hive Partitioning* can immediately be disqualified in favor of *Unity Catalog* and *Liquid Clustering*.
3. **Analyze the Spark UI Scenarios**: Be prepared for scenario questions describing uneven cluster worker utilization metrics. Remember that a disproportionate execution delay on a single worker node points immediately to **Data Skew**.
